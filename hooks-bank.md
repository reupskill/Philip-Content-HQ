# Hooks Bank

Ready-to-use opening lines, built from PB's own intake answers. Organized by pattern. Combine with the matching POV and platform template.

## "Most people think ___, but the truth is ___"

- Most people think real estate is for rich people, but the truth is that real estate is one of the ways ordinary people become wealthy. *(POV 2)*
- Most people think they're saving. If it's not growing faster than inflation, they're slowly losing. *(POV 3)*
- Most people think they're being careful by waiting. They're just buying at next year's price. *(POV 1)*

## "If you want to understand real estate, you need to understand ___"

- ...that land does not negotiate. You meet its price or you do not buy. The market does not wait for you to feel ready. *(POV 1/3)*
- ...selling, and what trust has to do with it. *(POV 5)*

## "One mistake I keep seeing people make is ___"

- Waiting for the perfect time before investing. *(POV 1)*
- Choosing a property because it's close to where they live, not where the value is moving. Sentiment is not a strategy. *(POV 4)*
- Asking a developer "How much?" before asking "What have you already delivered, and can I verify it?" *(POV 6)*
- (Realtors) Pushing the product before building trust. Sales without trust is just noise. *(POV 7)*

## "The real problem is not ___. The real problem is ___"

- The real problem is not that land is expensive. The real problem is that people keep using the word expensive as a reason to stand still while the price climbs. Expensive relative to what? *(POV 1/3)*
- The real problem is not the fear of being scammed. That fear is rational. The real problem is stopping at fear instead of learning verification. *(POV 6)*

## "Let me challenge how you think about ___"

- ...saving money. A naira saved in a bank account in 2021 is worth a fraction of what it was. A plot of land purchased in 2021 is worth multiples. That is not an argument against saving. It is an argument for where you save. *(POV 3)*
- ...being "too young" for real estate. Over 80% of our Uvest Metro buyers were between 20 and 29. *(POV 2)*

## Stat-led hooks

- Over 500,000 land scam incidents happen across Lagos and Ogun State every year. Here is how to make sure you are never one of them. *(POV 6)*
- Over ₦100 billion is lost to fraudulent property transactions in Nigeria every year. The fear is rational. The solution is verification. *(POV 6)*
- 80% of the buyers at our last allocation were in their twenties. Not wealthy. Disciplined. *(POV 2)*
