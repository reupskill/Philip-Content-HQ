# Template — Substack Essay (*PB on Real Estate*)

**Intent (per PB):** deep, data-backed editions. Field-tested, sourced. This is where the education engine lives: "The buyer who has been taught how to think about land... trusts the company that taught them more than any other company they have encountered."

**Length:** 900–1,600 words.

## Structure

1. **Title:** specific promise, no clickbait. e.g., "A C of O Alone Is Not Enough: The Five Documents Every Nigerian Buyer Must Verify."
2. **Cold open (2–3 short paragraphs):** a real scenario or story from the stories bank. Drop the reader into the stakes before any theory.
3. **The problem, quantified:** the relevant numbers from `07-market-data-facts.md` (e.g., 500,000 scam incidents/year; ₦100B lost annually). State why the reader's fear or hesitation is rational.
4. **The framework (the meat):** numbered sections, each teaching one thing completely — what the document/signal/step is, what it actually protects, how to check it, what failure looks like. Field-level detail. This section should be *useful enough to screenshot*.
5. **The deeper reframe:** zoom out to the underlying belief (positioning vs. preparation, trajectory over appearance, trust as infrastructure). One story beat here.
6. **Closer:** his closing-question pattern ("What decision am I delaying today that I will pay double for tomorrow?") + CTA: follow, subscribe, take one concrete learning step. "The first decision does not have to be perfect. It has to be made."

## Rules

- Every factual claim sourced from the knowledge base or flagged `[NEEDS SOURCE]`.
- Headers are plain statements, not puns.
- Write to one persona (usually first-time buyer or diaspora investor); name their situation early so they feel personally addressed.
- No section of pure motivation. Every philosophical beat is attached to a story or a number.
- End matter: subscribe line for *PB on Real Estate*.
