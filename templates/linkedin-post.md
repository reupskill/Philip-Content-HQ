# Template — LinkedIn Post

**Intent (per PB):** thought leadership and longer POV pieces. Founder reflection: short, personal, data-backed, ends with a question to the audience.

**Length:** 150–300 words. Line breaks between every 1–2 sentences (LinkedIn reads vertically).

## Structure

1. **Hook (1–2 lines, before the "see more" fold).** A correction, a number, or a signature phrase. Never a greeting.
   - Pattern: "Most people think ___. The truth is ___." / a hard stat / a verbatim PB line.
2. **Personal anchor (2–4 lines).** A real story from the stories bank, told plainly. First person, no drama.
3. **The reframe (3–6 lines).** The POV, stated in his correction structure. Evidence immediately after the claim.
4. **The practical turn (2–4 lines).** What the reader should actually do or verify. Concrete: documents, locations, calculations.
5. **Closer.** One aphorism line, then one genuine question to the audience (his style: a question that forces a calculation, not engagement bait).

## Rules

- No hashtag walls. 0–3 hashtags max, only if natural.
- No "Agree?" engagement bait. His closing questions make people *think*, not comment reflexively.
- Numbers only from `knowledge-base/07-market-data-facts.md`.
- Read the draft aloud as PB. If any line sounds like a marketer, cut or rewrite it.

## Example skeleton (Pillar 1)

> Over 80% of the buyers at our Uvest Metro allocation were between 20 and 29 years old.
>
> These were not wealthy people. They were disciplined people. That is a different thing entirely.
>
> [story beat] ... [reframe] ... [practical turn] ...
>
> The market rewards the disciplined, not the occasionally motivated.
>
> So ask yourself: am I preparing for opportunities, or am I positioning for them?
