# Template — Realtor Training / Sales Enablement Content

**Intent:** content for the Uvest Tribe, the Uvest Sales Academy, and realtor-facing public posts. The standard: "It is not enough that the CEO can win a conversation with a prospect. The entire sales team has to be able to win those conversations without the CEO."

## Format A — Training module (internal)

1. **The standard (one line):** what excellent looks like. e.g., "Serious realtors can explain why a location will appreciate."
2. **Why it matters:** the trust logic — "Information builds trust. Trust converts. That sequence does not work in reverse."
3. **The skill, broken down:** concrete behaviours, scripts-as-scaffolding (never scripts-as-crutch), examples of the conversation done well vs. done badly.
4. **Practice assignment:** something the Tribesman does this week (e.g., write a 3-line answer to "Why will Moniya appreciate?" without mentioning price).
5. **The measure:** how they'll know it's working (follow-up rate, education-first outreach ratio, pipeline consistency).

## Format B — Public realtor-facing post (LinkedIn/IG)

Uses the standard platform template, but the persona is the realtor and the POV is always one of:
- Consistency is operational discipline, not market luck.
- Trust before product — "Sales without trust is just noise."
- Knowledge over scripts — "Unserious realtors forward flyers. Serious realtors build conviction."
- Build a personal brand; teach before you sell; stop waiting for a company to hand you clients.

## Rules

- Never condescending. The tone is a top performer raising the standard, not a boss scolding.
- Every module anchors to a real sales situation (first outreach, follow-up, objection about scams, price objection, site visit).
- The scam-fear objection is always treated as rational — realtors are taught to answer it with proof and documentation, never dismissal.
- Funnel note: public realtor content may reference the Uvest Sales Academy / Uvest Tribe, but must deliver full value standalone first.
