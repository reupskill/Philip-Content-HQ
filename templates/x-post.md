# Template — X / Twitter (one-liners & threads)

**Intent (per PB):** one-liners and sharp takes.

## Format A — Standalone one-liner

One or two sentences. Maximum compression, no hashtags, no emojis. Either a verbatim signature phrase or a new line built in his cadence.

Patterns that work:
- The double-turn: "The risk is not buying. The risk is *not* buying."
- The refusal: "Land does not negotiate. You meet its price or you do not buy."
- The quiet math: "A naira saved in 2021 is worth a fraction today. A plot bought in 2021 is worth multiples. Where you save matters more than that you save."
- The correction: "Real estate is not about quick returns. It is about certain returns."

## Format B — Thread (5–9 posts)

1. **Post 1 (hook):** the sharpest claim, standing alone. Must work even if nobody opens the thread.
2. **Posts 2–3:** the evidence — a story or number from the knowledge base.
3. **Posts 4–6:** the framework — numbered, concrete steps or signals (e.g., the 5 verification documents; the infrastructure signals).
4. **Post 7–8:** the reframe — what this means for how the reader should think.
5. **Final post:** one closing question + CTA ("Subscribe to PB on Real Estate on Substack" when the thread is educational).

## Rules

- Each post under 280 characters; each must be quotable alone.
- One idea per thread. Threads come from a single POV in `03-core-beliefs-pov.md`.
- No "🧵👇" theatrics. The first line *is* the reason to read on.
- Sharp is fine; snide is not. He challenges the reader's thinking, never the reader.
