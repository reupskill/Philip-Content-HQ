# Template — Newsletter Edition

**Intent:** the retention channel. Deep, data-backed, personal — the reader should feel they're getting PB's actual thinking, not a broadcast. Pairs with Substack (*PB on Real Estate*); a newsletter edition can be the email form of a Substack essay or a standalone shorter edition.

**Length:** 500–900 words.

## Structure

1. **Subject line:** the promise or the tension, ≤50 characters. e.g., "The land they called bush" / "Expensive relative to what?"
2. **Opening (personal, 2–3 paragraphs):** what PB has been seeing, doing, or thinking this week — an allocation, a realtor conversation, a site visit, a market signal. Real, specific, dated to now.
3. **The one idea:** a single POV developed properly. Claim → evidence → what it means for the reader.
4. **"Do this" section:** 2–4 concrete actions or checks, written to one persona.
5. **One number worth knowing:** a stat from `07-market-data-facts.md` with one sentence of interpretation.
6. **Closer:** closing question + a single CTA (reply, read the full Substack piece, or one learning step). Sign-off: "— PB".

## Rules

- One idea per edition. Save the rest.
- Write like an email to one person, not an announcement to a list.
- No more than one link per edition beyond the subscribe/share boilerplate — attention is the asset.
- The opening must never be skippable throat-clearing; it carries a real observation.
