# Template — Video Script (founder-story & market insight)

**Intent (per PB):** video is for founder-story and market insight content "where his voice and delivery carry the message directly." Write for the ear, not the eye — short sentences, natural rhythm, room to breathe.

## Format A — Short-form (Reels / Shorts / TikTok, 45–90 seconds, ~120–220 words)

1. **Hook (0–3s):** the sharpest line first. A signature phrase, a stat, or the correction pattern. No "Hey guys."
   - e.g., "A Certificate of Occupancy does not guarantee that you are buying good land."
2. **Stakes (3–15s):** why this matters to *you*, watching — the fear, the cost, the missed signal.
3. **The teaching (15–60s):** one idea, fully. If it's a list, maximum three items on screen.
4. **The reframe (last 15s):** the one-line belief shift.
5. **CTA (one line):** follow / subscribe to PB on Real Estate / "start with one verified piece of information."

**On-screen text:** hook line + key numbers only. Captions always.

## Format B — Long-form / talking head (3–8 minutes)

1. **Cold open:** start mid-story (stories bank), 30 seconds in before any context.
2. **Context:** who this is for, what they'll be able to do by the end.
3. **Body:** 2–4 chapters, each = claim → evidence → what to do.
4. **The personal beat:** one founder-journey moment that earns the lesson (Eko Hotel, NYSC launch, the wristband failure).
5. **Close:** closing question + CTA.

## Delivery notes (write these into the script)

- `[pause]` after aphorisms — let them land.
- `[direct to camera]` for the challenge lines.
- Conversational contractions are fine on video even where written pieces stay formal.
- He never shouts. Intensity comes from stillness and specificity, not volume.

## Rules

- One video = one POV from `03-core-beliefs-pov.md`.
- Every script must contain at least one verbatim signature phrase.
- Never script hype ("This will blow your mind"). The information is the hook.
