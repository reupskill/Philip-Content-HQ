# Template — Instagram (primary channel)

**Intent (per PB):** visual, short-form, story-driven. Instagram is the primary channel.

## Format A — Carousel (6–10 slides)

1. **Slide 1 (cover):** one line, big. Signature phrase or correction hook. e.g., "Most people think real estate is for rich people."
2. **Slide 2:** the flip. "The truth is real estate is one of the ways ordinary people become wealthy."
3. **Slides 3–8:** one idea per slide, ≤25 words each. Numbers get their own slide (e.g., "80% of Uvest Metro buyers were aged 20–29."). Frameworks get one item per slide.
4. **Second-to-last slide:** the aphorism closer.
5. **Last slide:** CTA — follow @[handle], subscribe to *PB on Real Estate*, or one concrete first step.

**Caption:** 3–6 short paragraphs expanding the story behind the slides — written in full PB voice, not a repeat of the slides. End with his closing-question pattern.

## Format B — Story-driven single post / Reel cover

Photo or clip of PB on site, at an allocation, at The Jungle, at a training. Caption carries the content: story beat (2–3 lines) → reframe (2–3 lines) → one question.

## Format C — Quote card

A verbatim line from `05-signature-phrases.md`. Attribution: "— Dr. Philip Babalola". Caption adds the story behind the line in 2–4 sentences. Never invent new "quotes" for cards.

## Rules

- Slide text must survive being read in 2 seconds each.
- Design tone: clean, editorial, confident — not loud gradient-and-emoji real estate marketing. The visual should feel like the voice: direct, uncluttered.
- Every carousel maps to one pillar; rotate pillars across the week.
- Hashtags: small, relevant set in first comment or end of caption; never a wall.
