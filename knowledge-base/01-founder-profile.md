# Founder Profile — Dr. Philip Babalola ("PB")

Founder & CEO of Uvest. Real estate founder building in Nigeria: leading a team, selling properties, working with realtors, managing investors, launching estates, solving market problems, and learning from real experience.

## Origin & journey

- **Grew up in Oshodi, Lagos**, where "the visible markers of success around me were limited." Changing his context changed what he believed was possible — he now tries to be that changed context for young people.
- **Pre-Uvest ventures:** wristband sales (funded by a loan from his father — the business failed, and he avoided going home for a year out of shame; his father called him home, was not angry, and told him to learn from it), graphic design, and **LASU Resides**, a hostel agency at Lagos State University where the real estate vision first took shape.
- **Education:** graduated with a **third-class degree** after extra years in school caused by a lecturer dispute. He has disclosed this publicly on LinkedIn — it is part of his story, not a secret.
- **The 2017 Eko Hotel turning point:** attended a conference organised by **Akin Alabi** at Eko Hotel, Lagos. The speakers changed how he saw what was possible for his life. "They did not give me money. They gave me a picture of what was possible. That picture was worth more." He credits this as the defining turning point — and the model for the impact he wants Uvest to have.
- **The NYSC declaration:** declared to friends before launching that he would serve his NYSC year as CEO of his own company. He did not yet have a complete plan. He said it anyway. **Uvest launched 2020/2021 while he was still in the NYSC uniform.** People who told him he was rushing are now asking him where to begin.
- **Honorary Doctorate** from Estam University, Cotonou. He was initially sceptical, then moved when he arrived and saw a hall full of thousands of students.

## Uvest milestones (verified from intake — safe to cite)

- Crossed **9 figures in annual revenue**.
- **1,000+ clients served** and **3,000+ realtors onboarded** across five years.
- **13 estates developed and delivered**, including Millennial Woods, Milano Garden, Panda Estate, Oriki Acres, and **Uvest Metro** ("the City of Dreams", Moniya, Ibadan).
- **Over 80% of Uvest Metro buyers were aged 20–29** — not wealthy people; disciplined people. Many clients made their first-ever property purchase through Uvest.
- **The Jungle by Uvest** — Nigeria's first glamping resort, **4,000+ visitors**. Built because he believed Nigerians were ready to experience nature differently; the market confirmed it.
- Currently developing **Nigeria's first biophilic estate**.
- Named **Most Innovative Brand of the Year (2023)**.
- **4,000+ young Nigerians trained** through Personal Development with PB, the **Uvest Sales Academy**, and the **Strategic CEO Bootcamp**.

## The Uvest Tribe

A network of battle-tested realtors who have sold for Uvest, trained under Dr. Philip's leadership, and gone through his personal and professional development programmes. Called **Tribesmen**. Not simply buyers or clients — the sales force that carries Uvest's mission into the market. The Tribesmen with the strongest books of business are not the ones with the best scripts; they are the ones who became trusted sources of information first.

## Recurring convictions that shape his content

- Young Nigerians deserve a seat at the real estate table — Uvest exists for this.
- Nigeria's talent is real, but discipline is being eroded by a culture that celebrates wealth without work and influence over intellect (a theme he has raised publicly, including in a LinkedIn post referencing the Moniepoint/Tosin Eniolorunda situation, and from his own hiring experience).
- Established brands copying Uvest's content without credit confirmed Uvest sets the direction of the conversation in Nigerian real estate — and taught him to document origination deliberately.
- The founder who is the system is also the ceiling. Systems scale vision.
