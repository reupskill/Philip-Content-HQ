# Audience Personas

PB's content serves all of these; each piece should be aimed at one primary persona. He wants every reader to feel **personally addressed** — not just informed, not just sold to.

## 1. The Young Professional (primary — the Uvest Metro buyer)
20–29, earning in Lagos/Ibadan or remotely, currently renting. Spends on lifestyle (phones, outfits, subscriptions) and believes real estate is "not yet for me." Rational fear of scams. Needs: the positioning-vs-preparation reframe, the inflation math, proof that people exactly like them (80% of Uvest Metro buyers) already did it, and a concrete first step (learn to read a C of O).
**Feel after reading:** personally addressed; slightly challenged; equipped.

## 2. The First-Time Buyer
Any age, has saved something, terrified of losing it. The 500,000 scam incidents/year and ₦100B fraud numbers describe their nightmare. Needs: the verification framework (title documents, C of O, Governor's Consent, survey plan, chain of title), "buy what you can sustain — your first property is a foundation, not a ceiling," and the developer question: "What have you already delivered, and can I verify it?"
**Feel after reading:** more confident, less fearful, more decisive.

## 3. The Realtor / Tribesman
Selling or wanting to sell property, inconsistent pipeline, tempted by scripts and flyers. Needs: operational discipline over market luck, teach-before-you-sell, personal brand building, location knowledge (infrastructure timelines, growth corridors). Pipeline to the Uvest Sales Academy and the Uvest Tribe.
**Feel after reading:** more serious; challenged to raise their standard.

## 4. The Diaspora Investor
Earning abroad, wants Nigerian assets, burned before or scared by others' stories. Needs: remote due-diligence guidance, track-record verification, trajectory thinking (infrastructure signals), and evidence of delivery (13 estates, on-time allocations, verifiable landowners).
**Feel after reading:** reassured by proof, not promises.

## 5. The Investor / Wealth Builder
Compares real estate to crypto on speed. Needs the correction: real estate is not about quick returns, it is about **certain** returns. Trajectory over present appearance. Co-ownership, off-plan in growth corridors, eco-tourism/agro as the overlooked plays.
**Feel after reading:** more informed; sees signals they were ignoring.

## 6. The Entrepreneur / Business Leader
Follows PB for the founder journey, not the land. Needs: systems scale vision, founder-as-ceiling, consistency over intensity, depth before height, building people who don't need you, long-term health over short-term convenience.
**Feel after reading:** reflective; takes one structural idea into their own company.

## 7. The Internal Audience (Uvest team & Tribe)
Training content and internal memos. Needs: the standards behind the brand — education creates trust, delivery is marketing, documentation discipline, the conversation every salesperson must be able to win without the CEO in the room.
**Feel after reading:** clear on the standard; proud of the mission — "young people deserved a seat at the real estate table."
