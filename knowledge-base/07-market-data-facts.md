## Market observations (qualitative, his own)

- Housing costs increasing significantly and consistently; people renting more than buying — paying amounts that would otherwise service a land purchase while building zero equity.
- Growing demand for gated communities and luxury estates (security + amenities).
- More young people entering the property market than ever — an opportunity and a responsibility for credible developers.
- Sustainability is no longer optional in construction; demand for eco-conscious, nature-integrated development is real and growing.

## The verification framework (first-time buyer education)

Before any purchase, verify:
1. **Title documents** — the full picture, not one paper.
2. **Certificate of Occupancy (C of O)** — and know that a C of O alone does not guarantee good land.
3. **Governor's Consent** — what it actually protects.
4. **Survey plan.**
5. **Full chain of title.**

Plus the three buyer decisions: know *why* you're buying (build / hold / sell), understand the location's growth trajectory, and buy what you can sustain — "your first property is a foundation, not a ceiling."
