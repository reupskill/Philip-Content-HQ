# Content Pillars

Seven pillars, mapped to PB's documented POVs (see `03-core-beliefs-pov.md`), stories (`04-stories-bank.md`), and personas (`06-audience-personas.md`). A healthy month touches all seven; a healthy week touches at least four.

## Pillar 1 — Wealth Positioning (flagship)
Positioning vs. preparation. Time in the market. The cost of waiting. Inflation vs. cash. The friends who waited.
**POVs:** 1, 2, 3 · **Personas:** young professional, investor · **Cadence:** 2×/week

## Pillar 2 — Buyer Education & Due Diligence
The verification framework, C of O, Governor's Consent, chain of title, the scam statistics, "What have you already delivered, and can I verify it?", buy-what-you-can-sustain.
**POVs:** 6 · **Personas:** first-time buyer, diaspora · **Cadence:** 2×/week — this is the trust engine. "Education creates trust."

## Pillar 3 — Market Intelligence
Infrastructure signals (rail, roads, logistics hubs, Epe Agric Hub), growth corridors, trajectory over appearance, the 2019 bush land, co-ownership, off-plan timing, eco/sustainability demand.
**POVs:** 4, 12 · **Personas:** investor, diaspora · **Cadence:** 1–2×/week

## Pillar 4 — Founder Journey
Oshodi, the wristband failure, LASU Resides, third-class degree, NYSC launch, Eko Hotel 2017, the copied content, the Estam doctorate.
**POVs:** 9, 10, 11 · **Personas:** entrepreneur, young professional · **Cadence:** 1×/week — the humanizing layer that makes everything else land.

## Pillar 5 — Realtor Excellence
Discipline over luck, teach-before-you-sell, personal brand, conviction over flyers, Sales Academy / Uvest Tribe philosophy.
**POVs:** 7 · **Personas:** realtors/Tribesmen · **Cadence:** 1×/week (plus training content as needed)

## Pillar 6 — Leadership & Building Uvest
Systems scale vision, founder as ceiling, hard decisions, long-term health over short-term convenience, building people who don't need you.
**POVs:** 8, 9 · **Personas:** entrepreneur, internal · **Cadence:** 1×/week

## Pillar 7 — Mindset & Philosophy
Exposure sets the ceiling, personally addressed opportunity, consistency beats intensity, depth before height, the reward is bigger than the rejection.
**POVs:** 10, 11 · **Personas:** general, young professional · **Cadence:** 1×/week — always anchored to a real story; this pillar is where "motivational fluff" risk is highest. Follow the voice guide strictly.
