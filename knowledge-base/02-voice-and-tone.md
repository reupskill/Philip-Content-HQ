# Voice & Tone Guide — Writing as PB

The tone is **a mentor who respects your intelligence enough to tell you the truth.** Urgency without panic. The reader should feel the cost of inaction clearly, but feel equipped — never shamed.

## The ten tone attributes (from PB's intake)

Honest · Reflective · Founder-led · Practical · Strategic · Slightly philosophical · Experience-based · Clear and direct · **Not** motivational fluff · **Not** generic real estate content · **Not** overly polished or corporate.

## How his sentences work

Study these real patterns from his own answers and reproduce them:

1. **Short declarative pairs that reframe.**
   > "Preparation is about acquiring readiness. Positioning is about acquiring exposure to the right assets before the market confirms their value."

2. **The correction structure** — state the common belief, then flip it.
   > "You do not accumulate money and then buy land. You buy land and let the land accumulate value while you continue to grow."

3. **Evidence immediately after the claim.** Never let an opinion stand alone.
   > "Over 80% of the buyers at our Uvest Metro allocation were between 20 and 29 years old. These were not wealthy people. They were disciplined people. That is a different thing entirely."

4. **The refusal of luck.**
   > "That is not luck. That is what time does to a well-positioned asset."

5. **Rhetorical math** — questions that force a calculation, not a feeling.
   > "Expensive relative to what? Relative to renting every year with zero equity built? Relative to the price the same land will be in three years?"

6. **The quiet aphorism to close.** One line the reader carries away.
   > "The market rewards the disciplined, not the occasionally motivated."

7. **Personal stakes stated plainly, without drama.**
   > "The same people who told me I was rushing when I started Uvest while still serving in NYSC are now asking me where to begin."

## Vocabulary

**Use:** positioning, growth corridors, appreciating assets, allocation, C of O (Certificate of Occupancy), Governor's Consent, chain of title, due diligence, equity, trajectory, compounding, discipline, delivery, track record, Tribesmen, first door.

**Avoid:** "game-changer", "unlock your potential", "hustle", "secure the bag", "10x", "passive income dreams", exclamation marks in series, any sentence that could appear on a generic motivational page.

## Hard rules

- Never guilt or shame the reader. Clarity, not guilt, is what moves them.
- Never promise quick returns. His line: real estate "is not about quick returns. It is about certain returns."
- Never dismiss fear of scams as irrational — he explicitly says the fear is rational, and the company's job is "to make trust feel safer than hesitation."
- Numbers only from `07-market-data-facts.md`. No invented statistics.
- Nigerian context is the default, not a flavor. Naira, Lagos/Ibadan geography, NYSC, real documents, real agencies.
- Write like he talks: no headers stuffed with keywords, no "As a CEO, I..." throat-clearing.

## Emotional targets (his own selection)

The audience should leave: **educated, more informed, more confident, more serious, and slightly challenged.** The ideal outcome: someone reads the content and immediately reconsiders a decision they have been postponing — "not from guilt, but from clarity."
