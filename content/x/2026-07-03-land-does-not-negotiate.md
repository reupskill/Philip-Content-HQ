## Standalone one-liners (pick one per day)

1. Land does not negotiate. You meet its price or you do not buy. The market will not pause because you are not ready yet.

2. The risk is not buying. The risk is *not* buying.

3. A naira saved in 2021 is worth a fraction today. A plot bought in 2021 is worth multiples. Where you save matters more than that you save.

4. Most people are preparing for wealth. The ones who build it are positioning for it. Those are different activities.

5. People buy confidence before they buy property.

## Thread — "Expensive relative to what?"

**1/** The real problem is not that land is expensive. The real problem is that people keep using the word "expensive" as a reason to stand still while the price climbs.

**2/** Expensive relative to what? Relative to renting every year with zero equity built? Relative to the price the same land will be in three years?

**3/** In 2019 we bought land people called the bush. Today it is surrounded by development and worth multiples. That is not luck. That is what time does to a well-positioned asset.

**4/** Wealth is built more by time than by income. The earlier you position around growth corridors and appreciating assets, the more the asset does the work for you.

**5/** This is why 80% of buyers at our Uvest Metro allocation were aged 20–29. Not wealthy people. Disciplined people. That is a different thing entirely.

**6/** The person who waits until they are comfortable is always buying at the price someone else set years ago.

**7/** So ask yourself one question: am I preparing for opportunities, or am I positioning for them?
