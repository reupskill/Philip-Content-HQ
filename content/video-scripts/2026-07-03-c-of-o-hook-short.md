# Script — "A C of O does not guarantee good land"

**[HOOK — direct to camera, 0:00]**

A Certificate of Occupancy does not guarantee that you are buying good land.

**[pause]**

Here is what most Nigerian buyers never find out until it is too late.

**[STAKES — 0:05]**

Over 500,000 land scam incidents happen across Lagos and Ogun State every year. Most of those people saw a document. The document was even real. The transaction still went wrong.

*(On-screen text: 500,000+ land scam incidents — every year)*

**[TEACHING — 0:15]**

A C of O tells you the land has a recognized root. It does not tell you the story since then.

Three things it does not tell you:

One — whether the person selling actually has the right to sell. That is the chain of title.

*(On-screen: 1. Chain of title)*

Two — whether previous transfers were legally recognized. That is Governor's Consent.

*(On-screen: 2. Governor's Consent)*

Three — whether the plot you walked on matches the coordinates you are paying for. That is the survey plan, charted, not just printed.

*(On-screen: 3. Survey plan — charted)*

**[REFRAME — direct to camera, 0:50]**

The fear of being scammed is rational. But rational fear has a rational answer. It is not avoiding land. It is verification.

**[pause]**

Education creates trust. And the buyer who learns this once is protected for life.

**[CTA — 1:05]**

I broke down all five documents, step by step, on my Substack — PB on Real Estate. Start there before you pay for anything.

*(On-screen: PB on Real Estate — link in bio)*
